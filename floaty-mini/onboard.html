Floaty Mini Player is installed

One click to make sure everything is allowed. No digging through extension settings.

Checking permissions…
  • Alt+1 — mini window (whole page). On YouTube: video mini player.
  • Alt+2 — crop a part of the page into a mini window.
  • Alt+3 — show / hide the music disc (Spotify & YouTube Music).
  • Alt+4 — show / hide the compact music bar.
  • Alt+P — pause / unpause the music (or the YouTube video mini player).
  • Alt+V — volume up  ·  Alt+B — volume down.
  • Ads are skipped automatically on YouTube and YouTube Music.
  • Lightweight: it only builds its UI when you use it, and switches to a lite mode on low-end PCs.

You only have to do this once. If the button already shows “All set”, you’re finished.

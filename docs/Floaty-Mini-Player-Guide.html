Version 1.3.0

Floaty Mini Player

Pop any page into a small floating window, crop just the part you need, and keep a transparent music disc — or a compact music bar — on screen while you browse. Works in Chrome and Edge 116 or newer.

1 Install (one minute)

  1. Unzip floaty-mini.zip to a folder you will keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the floaty-mini folder. Done.
On first install a small welcome page opens with a single Allow everything button. Everything is granted automatically at install, so the button usually just confirms “All set”. You never have to hand-pick permissions in the extension settings.

Already had it installed? Open chrome://extensions, click Reload on Floaty, then refresh any tabs you already had open.

2 Shortcuts

Easy to remember: 1 = Mini, 2 = Crop, 3 = Disc, 4 = Bar, the same order as the buttons. Hold Alt with your thumb and tap the key.

No keyboard? Hover the right edge of any page for the Mini / Crop / Disc / Bar buttons, or click the extension icon in the toolbar to reveal them. The launcher can also be dragged anywhere with press-and-hold.

3 Mini window for any page

  • The window floats always-on-top and keeps the page fully interactive — scroll, select, copy, and click inside it. Links open in a normal tab.
  • Drag it by its title bar anywhere on your screen, even outside the browser, and resize it freely.
  • Works for Facebook, Instagram, PDFs, and text-only / solution sites. Sites that normally forbid being embedded (those that send X-Frame-Options or Content-Security-Policy) are handled automatically — Floaty lifts that restriction for the mini window only, and puts it back the moment you close the window.
  • The page itself is left untouched (the window shows its own copy), so nothing crashes or lags, even on heavy sites.
  • If a browser refuses the always-on-top window, Floaty falls back to an in-page panel with the same content, so you always get a mini view.

Crop

  • Press Alt + 2, then drag a box over the part you want (a chat panel, a paragraph, a table, a feed). Release and it opens.
  • Press Enter to open the current selection, or Esc to cancel.
  • If the always-on-top window can’t open instantly (the browser only allows it right after a click), the crop stays on screen with an “Open mini player” button — click it (or press Enter) and it opens. You never lose your selection.

4 Music: the disc and the music bar (Spotify and YouTube Music)

Open Spotify Web or YouTube Music in one tab and press play. The music UI is available on every tab, but it stays hidden until you ask for it:

  • Press Alt + 3 (or the Disc button) to show the disc.
  • Press Alt + 4 (or the Bar button) to show the music bar.
  • Press the same key again, or the little ×, to hide it.

The music UI does not auto-open on its own.

The music bar

A slim, horizontal player — handy when you want the controls without covering the page:

  • Thumbnail, song title and artist on the left.
  • A progress bar with the current / total time; click or drag it to seek.
  • −5s / Play / +5s buttons, the same dark / transparent / white background buttons, a ◉ button back to the disc, and an × to hide it.
  • Its own little wave bars that dance with the music.
  • Drag it anywhere and use the mouse wheel to resize.

Both the disc and the bar share the same colour and the same background choice.

Real, song-driven waves

The waves move with the actual music and voices, not a random animation:

  • YouTube Music — analysed directly from the audio.
  • Spotify (and other protected players) — Floaty captures the tab’s real audio in the background just for the visualiser (the sound keeps playing normally), then releases it the moment you pause or hide the player. If the browser won’t allow the capture, Floaty quietly falls back to a gentle animation.

Auto ad-skip

On YouTube and YouTube Music, ads are skipped automatically: Floaty clicks the Skip button as soon as it appears, and for un-skippable ads it silently mutes and runs past them, then restores your sound. Listen without interruption.

5 YouTube

On a normal YouTube page, Alt + 1 and Alt + 2 both pop out only the video (the browser’s native mini player) — never the whole page. Floaty deliberately does not rearrange YouTube’s page, so it can’t crash or lag it.

The popped-out video also gets its own small play/pause button in the corner, and you can always press Alt + P on the YouTube tab to pause / resume it. (Alt + V / B change its volume too.)

6 What’s new in 1.3.0

  • Much lighter on your computer. Floaty now builds its window, waves and music UI only when you actually use them in a tab. Tabs where you never open Floaty cost almost nothing — no canvases, no timers, no background chatter. The big win for low-end PCs and many open tabs.
  • Automatic “lite mode” on low-memory / low-core machines: fewer wave bars, a smaller drawing surface, no glow — same features, gentler on the CPU.
  • The player stops drawing when paused — no wasted frames when nothing is moving.
  • YouTube pause fixed — the video mini player now has its own play/pause button, and Alt + P pauses / resumes the popped-out video.
  • Gentler startup — when the extension updates, it attaches to your open tabs in small steps instead of all at once.

  • Progress + seek on both the disc and the bar — click or drag to jump around the song.
  • The waves follow the real music now, even on Spotify: Floaty captures the tab’s real audio (sound keeps playing) so the bars move with the music and voices, not a random animation.
  • Auto ad-skip on YouTube and YouTube Music — ads are skipped, un-skippable ads are muted and run past, then your sound returns.
  • The × close button is fixed and always visible — it can no longer be covered by the wave.
  • Background buttons on the music bar too — dark / transparent / white, shared with the disc.
  • Draggable launcher — move the Mini / Crop / Disc / Bar buttons anywhere.
  • Alt + 4 added alongside Alt + 1 / 2 / 3, and Alt+P / V / B.
  • Reloading the extension now updates open tabs instantly — no more “it looks the same as before”; the new version replaces the old one in place.
  • Everything from 1.1.0 remains: cropping on any site, video-only YouTube, no auto-opening disc, drag-everywhere mini windows, and one-button permissions.

8 Good to know

  • The transparent disc lives inside the browser. Browsers cannot draw transparent windows over other apps, so it will not float above your desktop.
  • Floaty stays light. It only creates its UI in a tab once you use it there, and it stops animating when the music is paused. On low-end machines it switches to lite mode by itself — same features, smaller waves, less CPU. Nothing to configure.
  • The always-on-top mini window is a real browser window, so it is dragged by its title bar (the browser does not let a page move it). The disc, the music bar, the launcher and the in-page panel drag by clicking and holding anywhere on them.
  • The waves follow the real audio. For Spotify, Floaty asks the browser to share the tab’s audio with the visualiser; Chrome may show a small “sharing this tab” indicator while it is listening. It stops as soon as you pause or hide the player, and your sound is never muted.
  • Spotify’s −5s / +5s is best effort and may need an update if Spotify changes its website.
  • Crop one specific area (a post, a panel, a table) for best results. Very large areas may look different in a narrow window.

9 Troubleshooting

  • Shortcuts do nothing? Reload the tab, then click the extension icon in the toolbar: you should see Floaty is active. Floaty attaches itself to tabs you already had open, so a reload is rarely needed. Reloading the extension also swaps the new version into open tabs automatically.
  • Updated but it looks the same? Chrome keeps older copies of the script in tabs that were already open. Just click the extension icon once on that tab (or refresh the page) and the new version takes over — no full browser restart.
  • Mini window looks blank or won’t embed? Give it a second — Floaty tries three ways in turn (always-on-top window → live view → in-page panel). On very unusual sites only the panel is available.
  • Music UI is missing? Make sure Spotify / YouTube Music is actually playing, then press Alt + 3 (disc) or Alt + 4 (bar). It is hidden by default on purpose.
  • The wave isn’t moving with the music? For Spotify the browser has to allow tab-audio sharing. If it was blocked, Floaty falls back to a gentle animation. Showing the player right after the music starts usually fixes it.
  • A red badge on the icon means the page blocks all extensions — browser pages like chrome://, edge://, the Web Store, or the built-in PDF viewer. Use a normal website, or the hover buttons on the right edge.
  • Nothing floats above other apps (games, Explorer, etc.) — that’s a browser limitation, not a bug. The disc and windows stay inside Chrome / Edge.
